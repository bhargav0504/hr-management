Standard Gujarat Leave Types:

  • PL — Privilege Leave (12–18 days/year, carry forward)
  • SL — Sick Leave (7 days, no carry forward)
  • CL — Casual Leave (7 days, no carry forward)
  • ML — Maternity Leave (26 weeks, Maternity Benefit Act)
  • EL — Earned Leave (as per Factory Act)
  • WO — Weekly Off (not deducted, for records)
